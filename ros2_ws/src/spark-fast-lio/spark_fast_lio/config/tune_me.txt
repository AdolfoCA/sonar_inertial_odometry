TUNING GUIDE — spark_fast_lio / ouster_os2.yaml
================================================

max_range + filter_size_map (VoxelGrid overflow constraint)
------------------------------------------------------------
PCL VoxelGrid computes a 3D integer index for each point:

    idx = ix + div_x * (iy + div_y * iz)
    where div_x = (2 * max_range) / filter_size_map

If idx exceeds INT_MAX (2,147,483,647), the index wraps around and points
get assigned to wrong voxels, producing PHANTOM POINTS at locations where
no real surface exists. This is silent — no error is thrown.

Safe combinations (stay below INT_MAX):

    filter_size_map    max_range (safe limit)
    ----------------------------------------
    0.2 m              ~63 m
    0.3 m              ~94 m
    0.4 m             ~126 m
    0.5 m             ~157 m
    1.0 m             ~315 m

INDOOR / LAB (room ~20m):
    filter_size_map: 0.2
    max_range: 50.0

HARBOUR / NEAR-SHORE (structures up to ~150m):
    filter_size_map: 0.5
    max_range: 150.0

LARGE OUTDOOR / OPEN WATER (structures up to ~300m):
    filter_size_map: 1.0
    max_range: 300.0

Note: points beyond max_range are excluded from the EKF update and KD-tree
but are still visible in the dense cloud_registered topic for visualization.
Set max_range: 0 to disable clipping entirely (risk of phantom points).


filter_size_map
---------------
Controls the VoxelGrid leaf size applied to feats_undistort_ before the EKF
update. Smaller = denser EKF input = better accuracy but higher CPU load.

    0.2 m   good for indoor / slow-moving platforms
    0.5 m   good for harbour / outdoor
    1.0 m   large-scale outdoor SLAM only


point_filter_num
----------------
Keeps every Nth point from the raw undistorted scan before the VoxelGrid.
Acts as a cheap pre-filter to reduce VoxelGrid input size.

    1   keep all points (recommended — let VoxelGrid do the work)
    2   halve the input (reduces EKF accuracy slightly)
